The Wrong Delivery

I had jokingly asked you to send me KitKats, never expecting you'd actually do it. That one small gesture made me feel cared for in a way I didn't expect.

The chocolates never reached me though. Instead, the parcel turned into condoms and lotion, and half the office had already opened it before I even got there. I still laugh thinking about how awkward it was trying to explain that it was actually from you.

After The Last Page...

If you've reached here... thank you.

Not just for reading this book. But for living every page of it with me.

While making this, I realised that memories aren't made from grand gestures. They're made from tiny moments that quietly become part of your everyday life. A morning call. A random Snap. A silly joke. A three-hour conversation that felt like thirty minutes. Waiting for your notification without even realizing you were waiting.

You became a habit I never wanted to break.

And maybe that's why this is difficult. Not because we're choosing different paths. But because somewhere along the way, you became home.

I know this is the right decision for both of us. I respect it with my whole heart. If loving someone means anything, sometimes it means letting them walk toward the future they truly want, even when every part of you wishes the road was the same.

I've seen you from closer than most people ever will. I've seen the version that laughs until her stomach hurts, blushes for no reason, says "Su nathi" with the cutest smile, spins around on her chair, and somehow makes an ordinary day feel special just by being in it.

Thank you for trusting me with that version of you.

If one day you hear someone say "Ho ka," unwrap a KitKat, lose an earring, see a sunflower, eat terrible pizza, walk on a beach at midnight, or someone casually asks you, "Su kare che?"...

I hope you smile. Not because you miss me. But because once upon a time, two people found something incredibly beautiful, even if they couldn't keep it forever.

You'll always be my 🐣🌻. And no matter how many chapters life writes after this one... this will always remain one of my favourites.
